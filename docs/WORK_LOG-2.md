Comparing `Current.png` against the live code found two real, concrete bugs, both confirmed against the exact real hull shown in the screenshot (`uw_tim`, the `underworld` mod, 25 weapon mounts, blank declared name):
  1. **Ship canvas near-unusable on hulls with many mounts.** `_add_packed_callouts` (the leader-line text labels stacked outside the hull, left over from the pre-redesign marker system) sprawled far enough past the ship on a 25-mount hull (confirmed: real mount locations span roughly -214 to 238 scene units in X) that `reset_view()`'s `fitInView` had to zoom out drastically to fit the whole label cloud, shrinking the actual ship -- and the new clickable mount boxes on it -- to a barely-visible speck, exactly matching what the screenshot shows. Fixed by positioning the title above the sprite's real top edge (computed from its actual height, not a guess) instead of at the origin; the bottom status note was likewise repositioned below the real bottom edge instead of a fixed offset that could clip a large sprite.
  3. Investigated why the screenshot's hull list showed many identical, indistinguishable `"CAPITAL_SHIP • underworld"` rows: confirmed against real data that this is not a bug -- `underworld`'s `uw_tim*` hull family (a boss ship's individual sub-module pieces, e.g. `uw_tim_u1234_f1_s1_w1_a1`) genuinely declare no display name in their own source data, since they're never shown standalone in-game. - 2026-08-24: Fixed SVG-016 (`docs/BUGS.md`) -- the ship canvas's weapon-mount boxes were positioned wrong relative to the sprite, confirmed by the user against a real screenshot ("the slots don't line up with the actual ship").

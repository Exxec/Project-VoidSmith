## Current Scope Boundary

Whole-fleet optimization is deferred.

The engine may record known fleet-support effects, but current optimization is
per ship. Faction analysis may still recommend individual native/retrofit/
foreign hulls (Phases 6-11) without that becoming whole-fleet optimization.
Non-faction equipment packs are supported through explicit provenance and
`UNALIGNED` affinity (Phase 8), not by inferring faction ownership from
source mod identity.

## Continuous Work Rule

Phase completion is a checkpoint, not a request for permission to continue.

After tests and documentation, continue automatically through the requested
scope unless blocked or complete.

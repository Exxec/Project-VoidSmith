**2026-08-26 GUI fitting follow-up:** the Ship browser's source-mod and
faction filters are now independent, based on explicit parsed faction
membership. The fitting canvas maps Starsector forward/lateral mount geometry
onto upright hull art and shows locally declared static weapon sprite previews
when available. Missing sprite metadata remains a visual fallback only; it
does not affect legality, scoring, or source data.
Carrier launch-bay, hidden, and decorative anchors are excluded from the
selectable weapon overlay while remaining available to structural/legality
consumers.
Carrier hulls show their declared bays as a separate non-selectable side stack
rather than as hull-overlay weapon markers.
Canvas navigation uses drag panning without persistent scrollbars, and generated
candidate previews share manual fitting's static-weapon-art lookup.

**Phase numbering note:** the pack's v0.5 refresh expanded this table from
23 phases (0-22) to 24 (0-23), inserting a new "Equipment Access +
Adaptive Autofit" phase (8) backing the new `EQUIPMENT_ACCESS_AND_
AUTOFIT.md` (equipment affinity/availability model, `EXACT`/
`STARSECTOR_STYLE`/`ADAPTIVE` retrofit substitution) and shifting every
later phase down by one. The pack itself ships this table as a blank
NOT_STARTED template on every refresh; the statuses and evidence below
are this project's own, reconciled onto the new numbering by hand, not
pack content.

**Phases 26+ note:** these extend the pack's own numbering with this
project's own priority list (a large, user-specified 17-priority charter),
tracked here rather than only in `docs/WORK_LOG.md` per the user's request.
Priorities 1-3 of that charter (scan lifecycle correctness, heuristic-set
consistency, the installed-mod acceptance sweep) landed earlier and are
folded into Phases 1, 2, 6, and 13 above rather than duplicated as their
own rows; Priorities 13-15 (profiling before optimizing, staged scan
progress, measured concurrency) are an applied methodology already
reflected in Phase 1/2's evidence, not separate deliverables. Phases 26-36
below cover the charter's remaining priorities (4 through 12, 16, 17) in
its original order. Per the charter's own explicit sequencing, Phase 35
(GUI modularization, priority 16) is intentionally last among the
concrete-deliverable phases -- "only then consider incremental
decomposition of main_window.py."
